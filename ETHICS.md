# Ethics statement

This software artifact does not contain a user study, newly collected human
participant data, credentials, private telemetry, or evaluator tracking.

The packaged CTI text comes from the public CTINexus research dataset and may
describe publicly reported organizations, vulnerabilities, threat actors, or
incidents. It is included solely to exercise and evaluate cybersecurity text
processing. Users should retain the upstream attribution and should not treat
automatically generated ANCHOR graphs as verified threat intelligence without
independent review.

The UCO, STIX, and MALOnt labels were authored by the ANCHOR authors for
research evaluation. They do not constitute personal profiles or operational
security determinations.
